Logo panel: four options

Every option does the same job: upload an SVG or EPS, set a size (at most 20″ wide × 3.5″ tall), pick Top or Bottom (1″ from the edge), and show the $20 logo fee. They differ in how the panel is laid out. SVG and EPS are vector files, so there is no background removal and no blur warning any more. The controls below really work, so you can try them in every option at once.

Try it
Board: 24″ × 36″, portrait, black. The sample logo is 4:1, so the 3.5″ height limit caps it at 14″ wide. Preview is full colour.

The placement rule, both ways

The logo is centred left to right. Its top edge (Top) or bottom edge (Bottom) sits exactly 1″ from the board edge. Width follows the size slider. Logo is 6″ wide here.

Found while reading v8 (affects every option)

  • Shoppers cannot reach the Logo section in v8 today. The Logo section is still in the page, but the three steps only list Documents and Size (Build), Board, Edge and Caps (Style), and the Preview items (STEPS6 in js/main.js). The build has to add Logo to a step. All four options below assume it goes in Style, after Caps.
  • EPS cannot be shown by a browser. SVG previews directly. An EPS needs converting to SVG or PNG to draw the preview (in the browser with a library, or by the Worker). The sketches show an EPS preview as if that works; the build must pick how.
  • On phones a section opens as a bottom sheet; on desktop as a card beside the board. Both are shown.
  • Today's panel (screenshots at the bottom) takes PNG, JPG, WebP, SVG and GIF, with a layout menu and six sliders. Limiting uploads to SVG and EPS removes the white-box problem and the blur question.
