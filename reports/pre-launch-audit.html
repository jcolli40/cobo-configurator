Pre-launch audit: what to fix before going live

Technical, accessibility, performance, and design/UX review of the new Shopify theme (preview 189807919179) and the v8 configurator. Restore point taken first: git tag pre-audit-2026-11-08 in both repos plus a full copy of the preview theme. Nothing below has been changed yet.

Top 3

Make the order actually match the board

Right now most v8 boards can't be added to the cart, and the ones that can don't tell Chris Corneliuz what to build or charge the extras.

  • Only 12x24 has Shopify variant IDs, and only for the 7 old color names; the 104 Wilsonart solids find no variant.
  • The order carries edge, caps and orientation, but not board color, laminate number or size.
  • Upcharges (laminate, wood/metal edge, brass caps) show in the price but Shopify never charges them.

Finish the homepage story and remove placeholders

The homepage shows "$__" prices and "Feature one / Feature two" in two separate pricing sections. It's long, has two US maps, and has no proof (reviews, price-from, lead time, FAQ). See the homepage sketch below.

One accessible brand blue, and a safety net for the 3D

White on #1B88D4 fails contrast on every primary button on both sites. A slightly deeper button blue fixes all of them at once. The configurator also needs a fallback when 3D fails (old iPhones, WebGL lost), and the photo-reader worker needs rate limits before launch.

Accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA)

MedThree H1 headings on the homepageLogo, strip hero and split section are all H1. Keep one.
MedImages and videos without text alternativesMade-in-USA badge has no alt; strip hero videos need aria-hidden (they're decorative). Footer copyright text is faint.
MedApp widget with white-on-white textComes from an app embed, not the theme; fix in that app's settings.
LowBuilder landmarksAdd a <main> and an H1 for screen readers on the builder steps (the welcome has one).
GoodKeyboard access in the builderEvery control reachable by Tab with a visible focus ring, on desktop and phone. Theme honors reduced motion and has pause buttons on animation.

Automated tools catch about a third of WCAG issues. Before launch: one pass with VoiceOver on an iPhone and NVDA on Windows through "build a board, add to cart, check out".
